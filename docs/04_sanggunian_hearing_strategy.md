# 4. Sanggunian Hearing Strategy — Questions and RONO Conditions

## 4.0 First, know your legal footing (read before the hearing)

> **Executive Order No. 32 (s. 2023)**, *Streamlining the Permitting Process for the Construction of Telecommunications and Internet Infrastructure*, lists **resolutions of the Sangguniang Panlungsod / Bayan / Barangay among the requirements that are no longer prerequisites** for shared passive tower infrastructure. The earlier **ARTA–DICT–DILG Joint Memorandum Circular No. 01 s. 2021** (revising JMC 01 s. 2020) also bars LGUs from requiring documents beyond its enumerated list.

What this means in practice:

- **Confirm with the Municipal Legal Officer and the DILG Provincial Office** whether a RONO is still a *legal prerequisite* here, or a courtesy endorsement the contractor is seeking (for community acceptance, a lease on LGU or barangay land, or the lessor's comfort). Your leverage depends on the answer.
- If a RONO is **not legally required**, a refusal may simply be bypassed, and conditions written as permit prerequisites may be challenged. Frame the council's asks as:
  1. **Conditions of a voluntary Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)** the contractor signs in exchange for the LGU's endorsement and facilitation. This includes lease of municipal or barangay land, road‑spur clearing, barangay clearances and power‑line coordination.
  2. **Matters still within LGU authority:** the building permit's technical compliance (OBO), zoning/locational clearance, road right‑of‑way, and the use of LGU‑owned property.
  3. **A public record:** a resolution *stating the council's technical findings* and urging DICT/NTC to hold the operator to coverage obligations is always within the council's power.
- **Lead with partnership:** "We will help you get the ridge site permitted fast (zero‑backlog one‑stop shop under EO 32), help with the access road, and endorse you to DICT." Make the better site the path of least resistance.

## 4.1 Five pointed technical questions for the councilors

Hand one question to each councilor. Each comes with what a good answer looks like and the follow‑up if they dodge.

**Q1 — "Submit the RSRP propagation map, for your site *and* the existing Bolod towers."**
> "Please submit your predicted RSRP coverage map for the proposed tower, showing the −100 dBm and −110 dBm contours. Submit the same map for the existing Bolod hill towers, with the tool and propagation model used, terrain resolution, antenna height, azimuths, tilt and transmit power. Then show us the *difference*: which barangays, and how many residents, get −100 dBm service from your tower that they do not get today?"
- *Good answer:* a named list of barangays with residents and a difference map.
- *If they dodge:* "Our independent analysis shows 75–94 % overlap and zero new residents. Please show us where we are wrong."

**Q2 — "Your antennas will be lower than the ones already there. What can they see that Bolod hill can't?"**
> "Your antennas would be at roughly 100–113 m above sea level. The existing Bolod antennas are at about 148 m, less than 2 km away. Name one unserved barangay — San Pedro, Pinamasingan, Mapanique, Santa Cruz, Ki‑Romero, Malaking Ilog — that your tower will reach and Bolod cannot, and show us the terrain profile."
- *Follow‑up:* "Did you evaluate any ridge sites, such as the Pinamasingan ridge on this same road or the Malaking Ilog–Mapanique ridge? Please submit your site‑search ring and the reasons each candidate was rejected."

**Q3 — "Who is the tenant, and why not co‑locate?"**
> "Which mobile operator(s) will be on this tower, and are they already on the Bolod hill towers? Who owns those towers? If the purpose is to bring in a new operator or add capacity, why not co‑locate on the existing structures, as DICT's common‑tower and co‑location policy encourages, instead of building a duplicate 1–2 km away?"
- *Why it matters:* if the answer is "capacity" or "a new operator," the coverage argument for a new structure disappears.

**Q4 — "What is your commitment to the 18 unserved barangays, with dates?"**
> "Is this tower one phase of a plan? Give us the rollout schedule, with sites, coordinates and dates, for the inland and southern barangays. What contractual commitment will you sign to on‑air dates and a coverage target?"
- *Follow‑up:* "Will you commit to a post‑construction drive test, shared with this council, within 60 days of on‑air?"

**Q5 — "Which frequency band will you use, and will it stay up when the grid is down or a typhoon hits?"**
> "What is the backhaul — microwave or fibre, frequency, link distance, availability target in our rain zone? What is the power plan: grid hours, battery autonomy, generator or solar? What is the tower's design wind speed under the NSCP, and what is your typhoon restoration time?"
- *Why it matters:* this is a public‑safety asset. The far barangays need a site that survives outages, and disaster coordination depends on it.

**Reserve questions** (if time allows):

- "Is this tower open‑access (multi‑tenant) at published rates?"
- "Will you submit an NTC / DOH‑compliant EMF exposure assessment for the nearest houses, school and chapel?"
- "Does the site require tree cutting or an access road on slopes that need DENR or MDRRMO review?"

## 4.2 Conditions to attach to the RONO, MOA or endorsement

Use whichever instrument the Legal Officer confirms is valid (see 4.0). Recommended structure: **"No objection, *subject to* the following."**

**A. Coverage‑extension conditions (the core)**

1. **Siting:** the new structure shall be on a site that delivers **RSRP ≥ −100 dBm** to at least **[2,000] residents of the 18 currently unserved barangays** who do not receive that level from the existing Bolod towers. Compliance is shown by the propagation study in B1.
   - *Pre‑qualified sites* (any one satisfies the intent, subject to survey):
     - **ALT‑A** Pinamasingan ridge, 13.0875 N, 123.0317 E
     - **ALT‑B** Laurente–Nazareno ridge, 13.1414 N, 123.0061 E
     - **ALT‑C** San Rafael–Santa Cruz ridge, 13.0697 N, 122.9636 E
     - **ALT‑D** Malaking Ilog–Mapanique ridge, 13.0075 N, 123.0556 E
     - **ALT‑E** Cueva ridge, 13.1017 N, 123.0578 E
2. **Fallback if the contractor insists on the deeper‑Bolod site:** the endorsement is for **capacity purposes only**. It must be paired with a **binding commitment to build a ridge site (ALT‑C or ALT‑D, or at minimum ALT‑A) within [12–18] months**, with the endorsement **lapsing** if that site is not on air by the deadline.
3. **Co‑location first:** if the stated purpose is a new tenant or capacity, the applicant shall first show that co‑location on the existing Bolod hill towers is technically or commercially impossible.

**B. Evidence before endorsement**

1. **Propagation study** (ITM/Longley‑Rice or P.1812, terrain ≤ 30 m), as in Q1, covering the proposed site, the existing Bolod towers and the difference. It is reviewed by the council's technical advisor.
2. **Microwave link plan** to Bolod (or fibre), with path profile, frequency, and availability ≥ 99.99 % for ITU rain zone P.
3. **Power plan:** grid connection plus **≥ 8 h battery autonomy** and generator or solar hybrid.
4. **Structural:** design per **NSCP 2015** wind zone for Masbate, signed by a licensed structural engineer.
5. **EMF:** compliance assessment for the nearest dwellings and public buildings.

**C. Post‑construction accountability**

1. **Drive test** within **60 days of on‑air** through all 22 barangays. Raw logs (RSRP / SINR) and a map go to the Sanggunian.
2. **Annual coverage report** to the Sanggunian for 3 years.
3. **Open access:** the structure shall accept other licensed operators on fair, published terms.
4. **Disaster readiness:** the site joins the MDRRMO communications plan. Restoration target after typhoon damage: **≤ 72 h**.
5. **Validity:** the endorsement expires if construction has not started within **12 months**.

**D. What the LGU offers in return (makes the better site the easy site)**

- Priority processing through the EO 32 one‑stop shop, with a target of zero backlog.
- Help identifying **municipal or barangay land** on the chosen ridge, and with right‑of‑way for the access spur.
- Coordination with **MASELCO / NPC‑SPUG** for a power extension along the Pinamasingan / Poblacion–Quintina road.
- A joint letter to **DICT** supporting the site for inclusion in common‑tower or connectivity programmes.

## 4.3 Draft operative clause (for the Secretary to the Sanggunian)

> *RESOLVED, as it is hereby resolved, that the Sangguniang Bayan of San Pascual, Masbate, interposes NO OBJECTION to the construction of a telecommunications tower by [Applicant], **SUBJECT TO** the conditions in Annex "A"; **PROVIDED**, that the tower shall be located at a site demonstrated by propagation study to deliver RSRP of not less than −100 dBm to not fewer than [2,000] residents of the barangays not presently served by the existing Bolod towers; **PROVIDED FURTHER**, that should the Applicant proceed at the deeper Barangay Bolod / Pinamasingan Road location, this resolution shall be deemed endorsement for capacity purposes only and shall lapse unless a second site at the Malaking Ilog–Mapanique ridge, the San Rafael–Santa Cruz ridge or the Pinamasingan ridge, or an equivalent site acceptable to this Body, is placed on air within [eighteen (18)] months …*
